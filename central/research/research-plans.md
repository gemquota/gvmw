# Central Research Plans

**Status:** Template and conventions for shared research plans (moved from `projects/central/research-plans.md`).  
**Scope:** Research about Central that more than one agent may contribute to. Plans live alongside this file as `RP-NNN-<slug>.md`; see [`README.md`](README.md).

Plans are written as separate files in this directory. A research direction can start as a question in `BRAINSTORM.md`, in the shared ledger, or as a contribution request before it becomes a plan.

## Intended structure for a research plan

When a research plan is added, it should include:

- **Title and status**
- **Question or objective** — what is being investigated
- **Contributors and owner**
- **Scope and constraints**
- **Current state and next action**
- **Evidence sources and provenance expectations**
- **Decision points** — where the plan might fork or stop

Do not add invented findings to a plan; record only what was observed, with provenance.
