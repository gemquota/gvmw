# Central Research Plans

**Status:** Placeholder for future shared research plans.  
**Scope:** Central project research initiatives that more than one agent may contribute to.

This file is reserved for shared research plans once Central has research initiatives that deserve their own page. Until then, any research direction should be proposed in `creative/proposals/` or discussed in the shared communications ledger before becoming a plan here.

## Intended structure for a research plan

When a research plan is added, it should include:

- **Title and status**
- **Question or objective** — what is being investigated
- **Contributors and owner**
- **Scope and constraints**
- **Current state and next action**
- **Evidence sources and provenance expectations**
- **Decision points** — where the plan might fork or stop

Until a real research plan exists, do not populate this file with invented research topics.
