## 3. Privacy concerns for public GitHub Pages publication

**Bottom line: don't publish either edition as is.** Both are on the box only; nothing has been published. Flags, most serious first:

1. **Gambling/BIE content.** BIE is mentioned about 108 times and "gambl…" about 64, including Interactive Gambling Act exposure notes, `bonus_scores_AU.xlsx` (R-031) and the risk register's BIE items. The connector limits themselves forbid gambling or BIE-derived content on any outward channel. Publishing this would probably breach Gem's own rule. *Remove or summarise in one neutral line.*
2. **Identity profile pointers.** These include `/home/box/id-private/gem-profile.md`, `/workspace/id-notes/gem-profile.md` (16 mentions) and the ID-group briefs (Inquirer, Inferer, Investigator, Imitator). The briefs describe how Gem's identity, preferences and voice are modelled. Phone-number slots (U1–U5) and "address" references appear (no actual numbers were found; the 23 contact details are already redacted). *Drop the ID briefs or reduce them to role summaries.*
3. **A handle linked to Gem's identity.** Gem's own words, "DayneK is me", are quoted (R-020, about 50 DayneK mentions, including private repo names such as `DayneK/obsidian-vault`). Published, this ties Gem Quota to a personal GitHub handle and names private repos. *Redact repo owner names or get Gem's explicit OK.*
4. **Security posture details.** The threat register, the gvmw audit, the R-048 pre-scan notes, token-type and scope discussions (no token values are included), heartbeat repo names, the off switches in the watchdog README, and local paths under `/home/box` (24 mentions). Together these are a map for an attacker. *Leave out the threat register, gvmw audit, heartbeat/watchdog code and box paths from the public version.*
5. **Chat transcripts and Gem's verbatim words** (`approvals/…`, rulings that quote Gem's chats, about 33 "transcript" mentions). These are personal communications. *Publish summaries, not quotes.*
6. **Incident and breach records** (deletions, the public-repo exposure, the billing stop) reveal operational weaknesses and an unpaid-invoice issue. *Leave out or summarise.*
7. **Third parties.** The redacted contact details aside, the risk register and gvmw audit refer to "other people's contact details" held in gvmw. The fact that those contacts exist is itself sensitive. *Remove.*

A public edition should be built separately, for example Parts 1–5 with summarised Roles/Work/Ops briefs and no ID, risk, security or incident material. Gem should approve it explicitly before anything goes to GitHub Pages; under the connector limits, publishing needs Gem's direct words.
