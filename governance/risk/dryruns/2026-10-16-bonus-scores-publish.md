Not charging for it doesn't help either. The legal call is Gem's and the lawyer's.

3. Attribution: it would be tied to whichever account publishes it (DayneK, gemquota, or a Google account such as slap.red.pokies), which links Gem personally to the promotion.
4. Platforms: gambling promotion may breach the content policies of GitHub, Google Drive or Vercel, which could lead to takedown or suspension of the account. I haven't verified this against current policy text.
