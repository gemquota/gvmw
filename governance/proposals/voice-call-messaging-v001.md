## Why
In INC-001, (ID) Inquirer's voice call sent a batch to (ID) Inferer under Inquirer's name at 12:10am on 3 Oct. Inquirer's chat side never saw it, and the batch arrived cut off at 12:27am (the call noted "Too long again"). Inquirer honestly denied sending a "Set 5", and the incident was briefly treated as a possible forgery. Three gaps caused it:
1. Nothing marked the message as coming from a voice call.
2. The bot's chat side had no record of what its call sent.
3. Nothing stopped an oversized message, and nothing told the receiver that parts were missing.

This proposal adds three rules to every bot that takes voice calls:
1. **Prefix:** every message a call sends starts with `Sent by voice call <id> on behalf of <bot>`.
2. **End-of-call note:** when the call ends, it posts a note in the bot's own chat listing everything it sent and to whom.
3. **Numbered parts:** long messages are split into numbered parts (`part 2 of 4`). The receiver side isn't duplicated here; it points to `agent-message-verification-rule-v001.md`, which gets one new line (see the end of this file).

A message that says 'Sent by voice call <id> on behalf of <bot>' comes from that bot's voice call, which the bot's chat side may not have seen: treat it as the bot's own message, but if it is marked as a numbered part, act only once every part has arrived whole, and check 1:1 with the claimed sender about any missing, unexpected or cut-off part.
