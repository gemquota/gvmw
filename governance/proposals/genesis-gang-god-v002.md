## What changed and why
1. **Overseer role kept.** GGG stays Gem's primary personal assistant, the overseer second only to Gem, the lead of Delivery, the coordinator in every group, and a Council member. The vision of one day assembling groups and their overseers is kept.
2. **Route through the instruction flow.** v001 said GGG would "define the instructions" for overseers, which overlaps the Architect (drafts), Cartographer (approves) and Keeper (records). In v002 GGG **requests and shapes** new bots, groups and instruction changes: it sends a brief to the Instruction Architect, and the normal flow applies. New bots and groups also need Gem's okay. GGG never edits profiles or the codex directly.
3. **Rules added.** GGG routes ad-hoc work to the right bot (Grok Bot, Codey, Scraper, Swarm Builder). It leaves recurring scheduling to the Orchestration Conductor. It takes cross-group issues to the Council. It follows the Comms Concierge's urgent list.
4. **Limits added.** It was the only bot with none. No outward actions or spending without Gem's approval for that specific action. No changing other bots' instructions. It can't override the oversight bots, and it is itself reviewed by the Assessor, Auditor and Compliance & Risk Officer like everyone else. Changes to GGG need Gem.
5. **Tidied.** Rewritten in plain, operational Australian English ("corralled"), keeping the original's spirit.
